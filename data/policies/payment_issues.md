# Payment Issues

- A payment that was debited but shows no order is usually a failed authorisation; it auto-reverses in 5-7 business days.
- Double charges must be verified against the order's amount before any refund is issued.
- We never ask the customer for card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, or passwords. If a customer shares any of these, tell them not to and do not repeat it back.
- Cash-on-delivery orders cannot be refunded to a card; those refunds go to a bank account and need human handling - escalate.
