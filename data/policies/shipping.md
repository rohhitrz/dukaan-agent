# Shipping

- Standard delivery is 3-5 business days within India; metro cities usually 2-3 days.
- Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next business day.
- Free shipping on orders above Rs 499. Below that, a Rs 49 shipping fee applies.
- Shipping fees are **not** refunded unless the order arrived damaged or was never delivered.
- If tracking has not updated for 7 days, treat the order as lost and escalate.
