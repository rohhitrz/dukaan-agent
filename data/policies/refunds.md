# Refund Policy

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.
- A refund can be issued once the order status is `delivered`, `shipped`, or `cancelled`.
- Orders still in `processing` should be cancelled, not refunded.
- The refund amount can never exceed the order amount.
- Partial refunds are allowed for damaged or missing items in a multi-item order.
- Once a refund is issued it takes 5-7 business days to appear in the customer's account.
- A single order can only be refunded once. If a refund already exists, escalate instead.
