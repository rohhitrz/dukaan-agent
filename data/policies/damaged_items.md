# Damaged or Wrong Items

- Damage or a wrong item must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery.
- Ask the customer for photographs of the item and the packaging before promising anything.
- For a confirmed damaged item: a full refund including the shipping fee, or a free replacement - the customer's choice.
- Do not ask the customer to ship a damaged item back; it is collected by the courier.
- If the reported damage is above Rs 5000, escalate to a human for approval.
